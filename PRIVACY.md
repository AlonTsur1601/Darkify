# Privacy Policy

Darkify does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal information,
browsing history, page content or analytics.

The extension runs locally in the browser. It stores only the global enabled
setting and per-hostname preferences in Chrome synchronized storage. Chrome may
synchronize those preferences between browsers signed in to the same Chrome
profile according to the user's Chrome sync settings.

To identify monochrome logos when the page cannot read their pixels, Darkify
may request a small copy from the image's original URL. These requests omit
credentials and are limited in size and concurrency. No page content or
analytics is sent to Darkify or any additional service; image analysis stays
in the browser and its temporary cache is not persisted.

Last updated: September 26, 2026.
